01 / Why it exists · Sherani, Balochistan, 2022

In eleven days a pine forest became ash.

12 May 2022 day 3
49satellite fire detections
4.9 km²burned so far

The fire started on 10 May in the chilgoza pine forest of the Koh-e-Sulaiman range, one of the largest stands of chilgoza in the world, on steep slopes with almost no roads.

15 May 2022 day 6
131satellite fire detections
22.9 km²burned so far

The front climbed ridge after ridge. Crews on foot could not reach the head of the fire.

21 May 2022 day 12
275satellite fire detections
36.4 km²burned

After a lull it flared again on 19 and 20 May and spread to a second ridge. Three people died fighting it. PAKFIRE exists so the next fire's path, timing and access routes are known on the first day, not the twelfth.

Detections: NASA FIRMS VIIRS (375 m). Burned area: MODIS MCD64A1, mapped burn dates. Relief: SRTM. Counts are for the frame shown and computed by PAKFIRE from those datasets.

02 / Heat that means something

Every kilowatt changes the plan.

Scroll to raise the fire's intensity. Flame length follows from it (Byram), and so does what crews on the ground can still do: the standard fire-behaviour interpretation used by fire services.

03 / What it does

Built for the hour the fire is reported.

01

Any place in Pakistan

Click a point, search a district, upload a file, or start from a satellite detection.

02

A second, then physics

Instant gives the footprint in about a second; Physics runs the full model on the GPU for up to 120 hours.

03

When it arrives

Time of arrival and intensity in every cell, hour by hour, with spotting and crown fire.

04

Who is in the way

People, kacha and pakka houses, informal settlements, hospitals, schools and power lines, with arrival times.

05

Ways out

Evacuation routes over the road network and the drive from the nearest fire station, with the time each road is cut.

06

Breaks that hold

Draw a fuel break or a treated area and compare the fire with and without it.

07

Fires burning now

Districts and hotspots from NASA's satellite feed, and automatic scenarios when a watched place lights up.

08

Every figure has a source

PDF reports, time-lapse video, GIS contours, and the validation record against 1,464 observed fires.
