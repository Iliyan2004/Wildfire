Quick footprint (about a second; full page at PAKFIRE Instant)

A real small simulation (the same engine, a 24 km window, constant weather of the current hour) contoured at 1, 3 and 6 hours. A fast first look while you are still placing the ignition; use Tier 2 for the full run.

Ensemble & burn probability

Repeats the Tier 2 run with perturbed wind speed (±20%), wind direction (±12°), dead-fuel moisture (±15%) and vegetation greenness, and maps the share of runs that burn each cell. The widths are assumptions, so this shows sensitivity, not a calibrated chance. Uses the weather and window of Tier 2.

Exposure

People (WorldPop scaled to the 2023 census), housing, informal settlements, hospitals, schools and power in and near the burned area of the open run, with the time the fire reaches them.

Evacuation & fire-service access

Routes from settlements out of the fire's path and from the nearest fire station to the fire, over the OSM road network, with the time each route is cut off. Needs the road database.

Run the fire model in the Fire tab, or open a run in Runs, to see what it reaches.

Fuel breaks

Draw lines across the likely path with the button below. A break is applied at least three cells wide (150 m at 50 m cells) and can be crossed only by embers. Uses the weather and window of Tier 2.

Fuel treatments

Draw polygons where fuel is removed or reduced (grazing, thinning, prescribed burning, cleared roadsides). The effect size is yours to set; nothing here is a measured treatment performance.

District discovery (national)

Rank all 164 districts by live NASA VIIRS satellite detections. Use a district as your area and start a fire inside it.

Fire hotspots (national)

The strongest or most recent satellite fire detections in Pakistan (375 m pixels, a detection is a fire somewhere in that pixel). Start a simulation from any of them.

Live monitoring

Watches the NASA satellite feed around a point. When a new detection appears within the radius it automatically runs a 24 h spread scenario from the strongest one and can post to a webhook. Experimental.
